Where can you find this stuff?

Thrift Stores

Difficulty: Hard
Cost: Low

You will have to sift through disorganized bins and shelves for hours to find something you might want to buy.

Music Stores

Difficulty: Easy
Cost: High

Music stores are organized and often curated. Therefore, it's much easier to navigate and find what you're looking for.

Custom

Difficulty: Medium
Cost: Medium (small invesment but inexpensive in the long run)

There is much more effort involved in making your own CD, but it's the least expensive option by far if you have the time to spare for it.

Online

Difficulty: Easiest
Cost: Variable

It's very easy to find music online. However, the price varies from site to site. Secondhand sites like Faacebook Marketplace will obviously be cheapest.
